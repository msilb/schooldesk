and fictional demonstration artwork. It does not vendor an OpenAI Sites starter,
school branding, school documents, source archives or third-party UI components.

Lucide icons include ISC and, for Feather-derived icons, MIT notices. SQLite
is public domain. Dependencies and their transitive dependencies ship their
own notices through npm; do not remove them from redistributed packages.

The lockfile pins the dependency tree used for the release. Dependency licences
must be reviewed again when packages change. MIT licensing for this repository
does not grant rights to private content imported by an operator.
